# Mega, Gigantamax and Primal are mid-battle transformations

Date: 2026-11-15. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/pokeapi-primer.md

- Mega, Gigantamax and Primal (Kyogre, Groudon) variants are not units. They are a mid-battle transformation of a base unit, as in mainline.
- The data pack attaches each Mega, Gigantamax and Primal variant to its base pokemon. The battle rules for transforming
  are a later slice.
- Regional forms stay separate units (2026-11-15-unit-is-pokemon-variant).
