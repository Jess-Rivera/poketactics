# Moving: facing choice, passing units, zoom

Date: 2026-11-15. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/select-and-move-plan.md

- After picking the destination and before the walk, the player chooses the unit's end facing (4 grid
  directions); the first one offered is the direction of the path's last step.
- Units walk through allies, enemies block, and no unit ends its move on another.
- Camera zoom is whole steps only, 1x to 3x (never below one sprite pixel per screen pixel).
