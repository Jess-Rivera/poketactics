# A unit is a pokemon variant, as in mainline

Date: 2026-11-15. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/pokeapi-primer.md

- A unit is a pokeapi **pokemon** row, not a species. Vulpix and Alolan Vulpix are different units with their own
  types, stats, abilities and moves, as in the mainline games.
- Species still carries the shared identity (dex number, evolution chain, gender rate).
- Still open: battle-only variants (Mega, Gigantamax, Totem) and cosmetic variants, which are not regional forms.
