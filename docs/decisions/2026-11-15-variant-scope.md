# Variant scope: defaults, Mega, Gigantamax, regional forms

Date: 2026-11-15. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/pokeapi-primer.md

- Units come from: every species' default pokemon, plus Mega (91), Gigantamax (30), Primal (2) and regional forms (Alola 19,
  Galar 19, Hisui 16, Paldea 4) variants.
- All other non-default pokeapi variants (135, for example Totems, Rotom appliances, Deoxys formes, Therian formes,
  Pikachu costumes) are left out.
- Mega, Gigantamax and Primal are transformations, not units: 2026-11-15-mega-gmax-transform.
