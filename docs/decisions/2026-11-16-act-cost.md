# Act cost

Date: 2026-11-16. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/battle-design.md

- A move's SR cost is derived from its mainline PP in bands (low PP is expensive), with per-move overrides in
  the move schema. Exact formula: [Open], a tuning item (illustrative: 35 to 40 PP is 2 SR, 5 PP is 6 SR).
- Acts that are not moves (push) cost a flat amount, [Proposed] 2 SR.
