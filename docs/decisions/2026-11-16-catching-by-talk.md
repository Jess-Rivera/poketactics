# Catching by talking (negotiation)

Date: 2026-11-16. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/game-design.md

- After a battle, the player may talk with a defeated Pokemon (like Shin Megami Tensei or Persona). Its nature
  decides which answers are right, and good answers improve the catch odds. HP and status do not matter.
- Only wild Pokemon can be caught; trainers' and bosses' Pokemon cannot.
- One catch offer per battle. The ball type adds to the odds.
- Dialogue design: [Open], decided in the catching sub-grilling.
