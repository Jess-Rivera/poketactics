# Design pillars: three equal pillars

Date: 2026-11-16. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/game-design.md

- Pitch: strategy RPGs (FFT, Fire Emblem, Ogre Tactics, Triangle Strategy) plus Pokemon, inside a run-based
  roguelite (Into the Breach, Slay the Spire).
- Three equal pillars, each aiming at a "that was awesome" moment:
  1. Tactical play is rewarded highly ("I didn't think I was going to make it").
  2. Boss battles feel like an intelligent human opponent.
  3. Environmental interactions: element combos on tiles (lightning on water makes shocked ground, fire burns a
     tree down) and terrain-type bonuses (Grass in tall grass is boosted until the grass burns).
