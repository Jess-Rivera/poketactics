# Enemy levels are fixed; over-levelling allowed

Date: 2026-11-16. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/game-design.md

- Gym, Elite Four and legendary levels are fixed and set the difficulty. Illustrative only: gym 1 level 6, gym 8
  level 50, Elite Four 1 level 55, Elite Four 4 level 70, legendaries 80+.
- Player levels carry between runs, and over-levelling is allowed (no caps, no scaling to the player).
- [Proposed] Risk logged: later runs may trivialise early gyms; difficulty modifiers (the fork's skull idea) are
  a candidate answer if that turns out unwanted.
