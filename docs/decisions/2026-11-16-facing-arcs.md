# Facing arcs

Date: 2026-11-16. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/battle-design.md

- Relative to the defender's facing: the attacker is in front if its sideways offset is at most twice its forward
  offset, behind by the same test to the rear, and on a side otherwise. Tiles exactly on the 2:1 line count as
  front or back (Jess's grid in the canonical file). Ranged attackers use the same test at any distance.
- Facing is its own damage factor on top of the mainline formula. [Proposed] front 1.0x, side 1.3x, back 2.0x.
