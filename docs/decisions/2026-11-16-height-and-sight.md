# Height and line of sight

Date: 2026-11-16. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/battle-design.md

- Higher ground gives ranged moves extra range and a small damage factor. [Proposed] 1.1x attacking down, 0.9x
  attacking up, per level, capped at 2 levels.
- Direct moves need line of sight, blocked by terrain taller than both units and by other units.
- Arcing moves (flagged in the move schema, for example Rock Slide, Sludge Bomb) ignore units but not walls.
- Fills in "reach and environment" from 2026-11-15-damage-and-hit.
