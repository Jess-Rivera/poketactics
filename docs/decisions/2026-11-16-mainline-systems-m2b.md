# Mainline systems: everything portable, in M2b

Date: 2026-11-16. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/battle-design.md

- The target is every mainline battle system that can be ported: status conditions, stat stages, type
  immunities, weather, abilities, held items.
- They get their own milestone, M2b, after M2 (battle depth) and before M3 (run layer).
- The order inside M2b, and what cannot translate to the grid (cut case by case with Jess's OK), are settled at
  its opening.
