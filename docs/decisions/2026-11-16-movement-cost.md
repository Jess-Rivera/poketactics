# Movement cost in strike rank

Date: 2026-11-16. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/battle-design.md

- A move action costs 1 SR per 2 flat tiles ([Proposed] odd tiles round up) plus 1 SR per elevation change.
- Difficult terrain sets its own cost per tile (illustrative: water 1 SR per tile, mud 2 SR per tile).
- No tile cap per move action; strike rank is the only limit. Climb 1 up and drop 2 down still apply.
- Updates 2026-11-15-movement-range (fixed 4 tiles).
