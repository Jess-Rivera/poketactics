# Starting a new save

Date: 2026-11-16. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/game-design.md

- A new save starts by choosing which region's starter you want; you receive all 3 starters of that region as
  the first team.
- You also receive 12 Pokeballs and some PokeCoins (amount [Open]).
- Before each run you choose your team and the Pokeballs on your belt. Pokeballs found in runs are kept.
- What carries between runs: Pokemon with their EXP, levels and evolutions; PokeCoins; Pokeballs.
