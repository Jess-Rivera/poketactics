# Battle objectives

Date: 2026-11-16. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/battle-design.md

- More than "defeat everyone", inspired by tabletop games such as Warhammer.
- Non-boss nodes roll one objective from a pool; bosses get fixed, hand-picked objectives.
- First pool: rout; defeat the leader (ends the battle at once, like chess); hold a zone for N rounds; rescue a
  Pokemon (with a significant catch boost); escape.
- M1 keeps rout only (2026-11-16-party-and-battles).
