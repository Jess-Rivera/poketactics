# Party size, battle sizes and battle end

Date: 2026-11-16. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/game-design.md

- The player carries 6 Pokemon in a run and fields all 6 in every battle.
- Enemy count varies by node; bosses (gyms, Elite Four, legendaries) use their full fixed teams.
  [Proposed] counts: wild 2 to 4, trainer and elite 3 to 6.
- Pokemon caught during a run go to empty pokebox slots; with no room the game says "Your boxes are full", and a
  warning is shown before the run starts if the boxes are full.
- Party members can be swapped with the boxes at heal nodes.
- M1 battles end on a rout (one side has no units left). Objectives come in the battle sub-grilling.
- Updates 2026-11-15-battle-scale (4 a side in M1 becomes 6 a side).
