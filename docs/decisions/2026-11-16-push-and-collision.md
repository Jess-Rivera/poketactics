# Push and collision; no lock-down

Date: 2026-11-16. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/battle-design.md

- Every unit has push as an act. It always moves the target 1 tile if the pusher's size band is at least the
  target's.
- Collision (into a unit or a wall) deals small damage to both. Pushed off a ledge: fall damage by drop height.
  Pushed onto a tile: the target picks up that tile's state (water, fire).
- No zones of control and no attacks of opportunity for now; revisit after the feel test.
