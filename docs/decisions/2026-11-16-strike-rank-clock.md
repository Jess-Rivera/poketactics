# Strike rank clock

Date: 2026-11-16. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/battle-design.md

- Base SR comes from a size band (height and weight together); Speed adjusts it by at most about one band, so size
  dominates and Speed does not do double duty. [Proposed] base SR spans a narrow range, about 1 to 4; big Pokemon
  are compensated by winning pushes; extremes are tuned after the feel test.
- Battle start: every unit is placed at its base SR. After that the clock runs and positions carry across rounds.
- On its turn a unit either moves or acts (not both). Its next turn = now + cost + base SR.
- A round is 12 SR. Between SR 12 and the next SR 1 is the upkeep step. "N turns" durations count rounds.
- Same SR: higher Speed first, then a seeded tie-break.
