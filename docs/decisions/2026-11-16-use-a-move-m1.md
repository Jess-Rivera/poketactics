# Using a move in M1 (#10)

Date: 2026-11-16. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/use-a-move-opening.md

- A turn holds at most one move and one action, in either order; choosing a facing ends it.
- A move may be used with nothing in range or in its footprint: a wasted action (later it can set up the
  environment).
- M1 resolves damage only, plus Self-Destruct's user fainting. Status moves spend the action with no effect yet.
- Multi-hit counts as one hit; fixed damage (Night Shade) is the user's level. One accuracy roll per unit hit.
- No line of sight, height, PP or SR cost until their slices (#17, #18, #13). Aim is one of 4 directions.
