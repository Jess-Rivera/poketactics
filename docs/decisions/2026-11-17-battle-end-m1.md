# Battle end in M1 (#12)

Date: 2026-11-17. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/win-lose-opening.md

- A rout ends the battle the moment the last unit of a side faints, even mid-turn. The result lives in the rules.
- Both sides wiped by one move (Self-Destruct, last unit against last unit): the side that used the move wins.
- Once over, no unit moves, acts or takes a turn. A stalemate has no rule in M1; the player restarts.
- End screen: Victory or Defeat, rounds played, units left per side; Same battle (same seed) and New battle.
