# Enemy AI in M1 (#11)

Date: 2026-11-17. Owner: Jess. Canonical: docs/discussion-outputs/enemy-turn-opening.md (primer:
enemy-ai-primer.md)

- One-turn scorer: list every stand cell, move and target, score each, take the best; deterministic, no rolls.
- A sure knockout beats more damage elsewhere; allies and the user count against an option; Self-Destruct allowed,
  its own faint counted as a cost.
- Always move then act. With nothing in reach, walk toward the nearest foe by path.
- Enemy turns pause on screen (camera, walk, area preview, strike) so the player can follow.
- The enemy side is always AI until #15 adds a hotseat toggle.
